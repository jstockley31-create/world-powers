Support World Powers

World Powers is free to play and run by one person. Donations pay for the server and keep the game growing. As a thank-you, supporters get perks for their nation. Your level is set by everything you have donated in total, so it adds up over time and never runs out.

Questions

How is my donation linked to my nation?

Sign in, then press a Donate button on this page. PayPal receives a private code that belongs to your account. When the payment completes, PayPal tells our server directly, we check the payment with PayPal, and your perks switch on within a few minutes. You don't need to do anything else.

I donated another way, or my perks didn't appear

Use "Claim a donation" on this page with the transaction ID from your PayPal receipt (17 letters and numbers). An admin checks it in PayPal and adds your perks.

Is this pay-to-win?

Perks are capped so effort still decides who wins: a set number of timer skips a day and a weekly treasury bonus. A supporter can still be beaten, and nobody can buy troops, land, or protection.

How does buying a Chaos world work?

After PayPal confirms your payment, a Start button appears in the Chaos world box on this page (and a reminder on your Account page in the game). Choose 15 or 30 minutes, the speed, how many nukes and the other options, then press Start whenever you like. The real world is saved the moment you press it and comes back exactly as it was when the time runs out. Once started it can't be stopped early or changed. Only one Chaos world runs at a time, with at least an hour of the real world in between, so if one is running you keep your purchase and start yours later. A refund takes back an unused Chaos world.

What if a donation is refunded?

A refunded or charged-back donation no longer counts toward your level, and the perks it unlocked stop.

Do perks work in seasons too?

Yes. Your level belongs to your account, so it carries over to every season and to new nations you found.
