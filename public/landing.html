A living world, not a board game

Every nation on the map is led by a real person or a clearly marked AI — and everything you do ripples through a shared world.

Real geography

961 provinces across a 3D globe. Oil in the Gulf, farmland in the plains, mountains and rivers that defend, islands only reachable by sea.

A real economy

GDP, unemployment, inflation and national debt. Set a budget, issue bonds, trade on a live world market — or go bankrupt.

Army, navy and air force

Strike any enemy, anywhere — by land, across the sea or over the ocean. A war planner shows your odds before you commit. Lose everything, and your nation can fall.

The World Council

Propose and vote on sanctions, embargoes and global treaties. Diplomacy matters as much as firepower.

Spies and elections

Steal technology, sabotage rivals and swing elections. Democracies face voters every few days — lose, and your policies are reversed.

Talk to the world

Global chat, private mail between leaders, and notifications on your phone when you are attacked or someone proposes an alliance.
